1. Introduction

RegMe is an event registration and attendee management platform that enables event organisers to register participants, manage registrations, verify payments and communicate event-related information.

RegMe is operated by Avtech Solutions

This Privacy Policy explains how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared and protected when individuals use RegMe's registration forms, website and related services, including WhatsApp integration where enabled.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on the event and features enabled, RegMe may collect:

  • Personal information, including names, telephone numbers, email addresses and other details requested during registration.
  • Event information, including event selection, registration status and attendee category.
  • Payment information, including transaction references, payment status, payment methods and uploaded proof of payment.
  • Communication information associated with registration and event-related messages.
  • Technical information, such as IP addresses, browser information and system logs, where collected.
  • Administrative records needed to manage registrations, verify payments and maintain platform security.

The information collected depends on the requirements of each event and the services being used.

3. How We Use Your Information

Personal information may be used to:

  • Create and manage event registrations.
  • Send registration confirmations and event updates.
  • Verify payments and reconcile payment proofs.
  • Help event organisers manage attendee lists and attendance.
  • Send WhatsApp notifications where integration is enabled.
  • Maintain platform security and prevent fraudulent activity.
  • Troubleshoot technical problems and improve service reliability.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

RegMe does not sell personal information to third parties.

4. Data Controller and Responsibilities

The event organiser is the data controller for personal information collected and processed for the purpose of managing its event, including registration, attendee administration and payment verification.

The event organiser determines the purposes for which participant information is collected and how it is used for that event.

RegMe provides the technology platform used to facilitate registration and event administration. Depending on the services and arrangements involved, RegMe or its operator may process personal information on behalf of the event organiser.

Event organisers are responsible for ensuring that their collection and use of personal information complies with applicable privacy and data protection requirements.

For privacy enquiries relating to a particular event, participants may contact the event organiser or email ashamunemo@gmail.com for assistance.

5. Sharing of Information

Personal information may be shared with:

  • Event organisers, to manage registrations, verify payments and administer events.
  • Hosting providers, to store and serve the platform and its data.
  • Meta and WhatsApp, where their services are integrated into registration and communication processes.
  • Other technical service providers, where necessary to operate and maintain the platform.
  • Authorities, where disclosure is required by applicable law.

Information is shared only as necessary for the relevant purpose and subject to applicable legal requirements.

6. Meta Graph API and WhatsApp Integration

RegMe uses the Meta Graph API for supported integrations with Meta services. Where WhatsApp integration is enabled, RegMe may process telephone numbers, registration details and relevant communication information to provide registration confirmations, payment notifications and event announcements.

The specific information processed depends on the Meta products, permissions and features enabled. Meta's own terms and privacy policies also apply to its services.

For more information, visit the Meta Privacy Policy: https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/

7. Data Storage, Hosting and Security

RegMe is hosted by Host Africa, with the hosting and storage infrastructure located in South Africa.

Personal information may therefore be stored and processed in South Africa, subject to the hosting arrangements and services used.

RegMe uses technical and organisational measures intended to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, loss, disclosure and misuse. These measures may include authentication, access controls, secure connections, database permissions and backups, depending on the deployment.

No electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

RegMe retains event registration information for the duration of the registration period, throughout the event, and for one month after the event concludes.

The additional one-month retention period is intended to allow attendees and event organisers to resolve post-event queries and outstanding matters, including:

  • Payment verification and reconciliation.
  • Refund requests and refund-related enquiries.
  • Registration errors or disputes.
  • Questions concerning attendance or event participation.
  • Other event-related complaints, clarifications or administrative matters.

At the end of this period, personal information will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate, unless further retention is necessary to resolve an outstanding query or dispute, comply with applicable legal obligations, maintain required financial records or establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

Where additional retention is necessary, the relevant information will be retained only for as long as reasonably required for that purpose, subject to applicable law and appropriate safeguards.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may request to:

  • Access personal information held about them.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Request deletion of personal information.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.
  • Raise a complaint with the relevant data protection authority.

Requests can be submitted to ashamunemo@gmail.com. Identity verification may be required before a request is fulfilled.

Certain information may need to be retained where required by law or for legitimate recordkeeping purposes.

10. Children's Privacy

Event organisers are responsible for identifying age restrictions and ensuring appropriate safeguards where registrations involve children.

Where required, appropriate parental or guardian consent must be obtained before processing children's personal information.

Please contact the privacy email listed in this policy if you have concerns about a child's personal information.

11. International Data Transfers

RegMe's hosting and storage infrastructure is located in South Africa. Information may also be processed by third-party service providers, including Meta, in other locations depending on the services used.

Applicable data protection requirements and safeguards must be considered for any international transfer of personal information.

The specific obligations depend on the activities undertaken, the parties involved and applicable law.
